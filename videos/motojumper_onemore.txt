One More Balloon! 🎈 First Math for Kids #shorts

BESCHREIBUNG:
Juju the bunny has one balloon. Here comes one more! How many are there now? And what happens when one balloon flies away? Count along with Juju!

Learning goals: one more and one less, numbers 1 to 3.

🌈 A new learning story every day on Motojumper, with Momo, Juju and friends!

#shorts #mathforkids #countingforkids #kidslearning

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Juju has one balloon.
Sunny: Here comes one more. One, and one more, makes...
Juju: Two!
Sunny: And one more! Two, and one more, makes...
Juju: Three!
Sunny: Oh! One balloon flies away. Three, take away one, is...
Juju: Two! Bye bye, balloon!
Sunny: One more, and one less. You can do math! Bye bye!
