BESCHREIBUNG:
Freddy the frog wants to visit his friend Kiki, but the pond is so big! Count the jumps with Freddy: one, two, three! A little story about being brave.

Learning goals: counting to 3, trying even when you are a little scared.

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Freddy the frog wants to visit Kiki.
Kiki: Come over, Freddy!
Freddy: But the pond is so big. I am a little scared.
Sunny: You can do it, Freddy! Let's count the jumps.
Sunny: One!
Sunny: Two!
Sunny: Three!
Freddy: One more jump!
Freddy: I did it! I did it!
Kiki: Hooray, Freddy! You are so brave!
Sunny: Freddy was scared, but he tried. Great job! Bye bye!
