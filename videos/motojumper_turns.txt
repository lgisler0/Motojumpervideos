DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Dodo and Kiki both want the ball.
Dodo: I want it!
Kiki: No, I want it!
Sunny: Oh dear. What can they do? They can take turns!
Sunny: First, it is Dodo's turn.
Sunny: Then, it is Kiki's turn.
Dodo: This is fun!
Kiki: Yes! Playing together is the best!
Sunny: Taking turns is fair. Great job, friends! Bye bye!
