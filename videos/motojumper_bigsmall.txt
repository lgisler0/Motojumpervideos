Momo the bear is big. Pip the chick is small. Pip wants an apple, but it hangs too high! Can big Momo help his small friend?

Learning goals: big and small, high and low, helping each other.

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! This is Momo. Momo is big.
Sunny: And this is Pip. Pip is small.
Pip: I want that apple! But it is too high!
Sunny: Pip jumps, and jumps. But Pip is too small.
Momo: Don't worry, Pip! I am big. I can help you!
Sunny: Momo shakes the tree.
Sunny: Now the apple is low.
Pip: Thank you, Momo! You are a good friend!
Sunny: Big friends and small friends help each other. Great job!
Sunny: Bye bye! See you next time!
