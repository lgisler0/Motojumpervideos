Tag und Nacht: was wir am Tag und in der Nacht machen

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! It is day. Momo plays in the sun.
Momo: I love the day! I can run and play!
Sunny: Now the sun goes down. It is getting dark.
Momo: Yawn! I am sleepy.
Sunny: Look up! The moon and the stars. Now it is night.
Sunny: At night, we go to bed.
Momo: Good night, moon! Good night, stars!
Sunny: Shh. Momo is sleeping.
Sunny: Good morning! The sun comes up. It is day again!
Momo: Good morning, Sunny!
Sunny: Day and night. What do you do at night? Bye bye!
