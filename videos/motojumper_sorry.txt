BESCHREIBUNG:
Kiki the cat builds a tall tower. Then Dodo the dog runs by, and oops, the tower falls down! What does a good friend do now?

Learning goals: saying sorry and helping to fix a mistake.

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Kiki builds a tall tower.
Kiki: One, two, three blocks! So tall!
Sunny: Here comes Dodo. He runs very fast.
Sunny: Oops! The tower falls down.
Kiki: Oh no! My tower!
Dodo: I am sorry, Kiki. It was an accident.
Kiki: It's okay, Dodo.
Sunny: Dodo helps to build it again.
Sunny: When we make a mistake, we say sorry. Good friends! Bye bye!
