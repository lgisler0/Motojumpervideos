TITEL:
Kiki Jumps In the Box! 📦 In and Out #shorts

BESCHREIBUNG:
Kiki the cat found a big box, and cats love boxes! She jumps in. She jumps out. In, out, in, out. Where is Kiki now?

Learning goals: in and out.

🌈 A new learning story every day on Motojumper, with Momo, Juju and friends!

#shorts #inandout #oppositesforkids #kidslearning

TAGS:
in and out, opposites for kids, cat in a box, position words

LERNZIEL:
Gegensatz: drin und draussen (in / out)

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Kiki found a big box.
Kiki: I love boxes! Meow!
Sunny: Kiki jumps in the box.
Sunny: Kiki jumps out of the box.
Sunny: In!
Sunny: Out!
Sunny: Where is Kiki now?
Kiki: I am in the box! Peekaboo!
Sunny: In and out. Great job! Bye bye!
