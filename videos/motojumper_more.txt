Who Has More Apples? 🍎 More, Fewer, the Same #shorts

BESCHREIBUNG:
Momo the bear has three apples. Juju the bunny has only one. Who has more? And what can Momo do to make it fair?

Learning goals: more, fewer and the same.

TAGS:

LERNZIEL:
Mengen vergleichen: mehr, weniger, gleich viel

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Momo and Juju have apples.
Sunny: Momo has three apples. Juju has one apple.
Sunny: Who has more?
Sunny: Momo has more! Juju has fewer.
Momo: That is not fair. Here, Juju!
Sunny: Now Momo has two, and Juju has two. That is the same!
Juju: Thank you, Momo!
Sunny: More, fewer, the same. Great job! Bye bye!
