Kiki Builds a House 🏠 Learn Shapes | Story for Kids #shorts

BESCHREIBUNG:
Kiki the cat wants to build a house. Help her find the right shapes: a square for the walls, a triangle for the roof and a circle for the window!

Learning goals: square, triangle and circle.

Sunny: Hi friends! This is Kiki the cat.
Kiki: Hello! I want to build a house!
Sunny: Let's help Kiki! First, we need a square.
Sunny: A square has four sides.
Sunny: Now a triangle, for the roof.
Sunny: A triangle has three sides.
Sunny: And a circle, for the window.
Kiki: A circle is round!
Sunny: Square, triangle, circle. Look! A house!
Kiki: I love my house! Thank you, friends!
