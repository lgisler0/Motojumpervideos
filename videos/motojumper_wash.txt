Sunny: Hi friends! Momo played in the mud. Look at him!
Momo: I am hungry! Can I eat an apple now?
Juju: Wait, Momo! First, we wash our hands!
Sunny: Turn on the water. Make your hands wet.
Sunny: Now the soap. Scrub, scrub, scrub!
Sunny: Scrub the front, the back, and between your fingers.
Sunny: Rinse the soap away.
Sunny: Turn off the water, and dry your hands.
Momo: Clean hands! Now I can eat my apple!
Sunny: Wet, soap, scrub, rinse, dry. Great job! Bye bye!
