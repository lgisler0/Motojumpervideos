Learning goals: the colors red, blue and yellow, saying thank you.

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Juju the bunny has a red balloon.
Juju: I love my red balloon!
Sunny: Oh no! The wind blows the balloon away!
Juju: My balloon! Where is it?
Sunny: Let's help Juju. Is this Juju's balloon?
Juju: No. That one is blue.
Sunny: Is this Juju's balloon?
Juju: No. That one is yellow.
Sunny: Look up there! Is this Juju's balloon?
Juju: Yes! That is my red balloon!
Juju: Thank you, friends!
Sunny: We found the red balloon. Great job! Bye bye!
