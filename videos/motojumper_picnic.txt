DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Momo and Juju have a picnic today.
Juju: What is in the basket, Momo?
Momo: Let's look!
Sunny: It is red, and round. It's an apple!
Sunny: It is long, and yellow. It's a banana!
Sunny: It is round, and orange. It's an orange!
Juju: Apple, banana, orange. Yummy fruit!
Momo: Fruit makes us strong!
Sunny: What is your favorite fruit? Bye bye, friends!
