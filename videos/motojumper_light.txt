TITEL:
Red Means Stop, Green Means Go! 🚦 Traffic Light for Kids #shorts

BESCHREIBUNG:
Juju the bunny goes for a walk and finds a traffic light. Red means stop, yellow means wait, green means go! Play along with Juju: what do you do when the light is red?

Learning goals: traffic light colors and what they mean.

#shorts #kidslearning #storyforkids #preschool

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Juju goes for a walk. Look! A traffic light!
Sunny: Red means stop!
Juju: Red! I stop.
Sunny: Yellow means wait.
Juju: Yellow! I wait.
Sunny: Green means go!
Juju: Green! I go! Hop, hop, hop!
Sunny: Let's play again! The light is red.
Sunny: What does Juju do?
Juju: I stop!
Sunny: Yes! Red means stop, yellow means wait, green means go. Great job! Bye bye!
