DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Shh. Pip is sleeping.
Kiki: We must be quiet.
Sunny: Here comes Dodo.
Dodo: Woof! Woof! Let's play!
Sunny: Oh no! That was loud. Pip woke up!
Dodo: Sorry, Pip. I will be quiet now.
Sunny: Dodo is as quiet as a mouse. Pip goes back to sleep.
Sunny: Loud and quiet. When a friend sleeps, we are quiet. Bye bye!
