BESCHREIBUNG:
😊 Juju the bunny builds a tall tower and she is happy. But then the tower falls down, and Juju is sad. 😢 Can Dodo the dog help? And what surprise is waiting at the end?

✨ What you learn: the feelings happy, sad and surprised, and how friends help.

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Juju builds a tall tower.
Juju: Look at my tower! I am happy!
Sunny: Oh no! The tower falls down!
Juju: My tower! I am sad.
Sunny: Here comes Dodo.
Dodo: Don't be sad, Juju. Let's build it again, together!
Sunny: One, two, three, four. Up it goes!
Juju: Our tower is even taller! I am happy again!
Sunny: And look! Dodo has a surprise.
Juju: Wow! A star! I am surprised!
Sunny: Happy, sad, surprised. How do you feel today? Bye bye!
