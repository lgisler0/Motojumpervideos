DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Dodo goes on a big trip. First, he drives a car!
Dodo: Vroom, vroom! A car drives on the road.
Sunny: Now Dodo comes to the water. A car cannot swim!
Sunny: So Dodo takes a boat.
Dodo: Toot, toot! A boat floats on the water.
Sunny: Now Dodo wants to go up, up, high in the sky!
Sunny: So Dodo takes a plane!
Dodo: Zoom! A plane flies in the sky. Wheee!
Sunny: A car on the road, a boat on the water, and a plane in the sky. Great trip, Dodo! Bye bye!
