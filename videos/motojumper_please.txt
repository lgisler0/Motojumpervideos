Sunny: Hi friends! Momo has a basket of fruit.
Pip: Banana! Give me the banana!
Momo: Hmm...
Sunny: Pip, what is the magic word?
Pip: Oh! May I have the banana, please?
Momo: Of course! Here you go, Pip.
Pip: Thank you, Momo!
Sunny: Here comes Kiki.
Kiki: Momo, may I have an orange, please?
Momo: Yes! You asked so nicely.
Kiki: Thank you!
Sunny: Please and thank you are magic words. Great job! Bye bye!
