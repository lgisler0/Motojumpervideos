Where Is Dodo's Ball? ⚽ On, Next To, Behind | Story for Kids #shorts

BESCHREIBUNG:
Dodo the dog loves his red ball. Boing! Where does it bounce? On the box, next to the box, or behind the bush? Look closely and help Dodo find it!

Learning goals: the position words on, next to and behind.

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! Dodo the dog loves his red ball.
Dodo: Woof! Let's play!
Sunny: Boing! The ball is on the box.
Sunny: Boing! Now the ball is next to the box.
Sunny: Boing! Oh! Where is the ball now?
Dodo: Hmm. Where is my ball?
Sunny: Look closely! The ball is behind the bush!
Dodo: I found it! Woof, woof!
Sunny: On, next to, behind. Great looking! Bye bye!
