TITEL:
Momo Shares His Apples 🍎 Count to 5 | Story for Kids #shorts

BESCHREIBUNG:
Momo the bear is hungry and finds an apple tree. Count the apples with Momo: one, two, three, four, five! Then his friend Juju the bunny comes along, and Momo learns that sharing is caring.

Learning goals: counting 1 to 5, sharing with friends.

🌈 A new learning story every day on Motojumper, with Momo, Juju and friends!

DREHBUCH:
Sunny: Hi friends! This is Momo the bear.
Momo: Hello! I am so hungry!
Sunny: Look, Momo! An apple tree! Let's count the apples.
Sunny: One!
Sunny: Two!
Sunny: Three!
Sunny: Four!
Sunny: Five!
Momo: Five apples! Yummy!
Sunny: Oh look! Here comes Juju the bunny.
Juju: Hello Momo! I am hungry too.
Sunny: Hmm. What can Momo do?
Momo: Here, Juju! Let's share!
Sunny: Two apples for Juju, and three apples for Momo.
Juju: Thank you, Momo!
Sunny: Sharing is caring! Great job, Momo!
Sunny: Bye bye, friends! See you next time!
