How fast are you moving right now?
The Earth spins. At the equator, the ground moves at about 1,670 kilometers per hour.
At the same time, we race around the Sun at 107,000 kilometers per hour.
And the whole solar system orbits the galaxy at roughly 800,000 kilometers per hour.
