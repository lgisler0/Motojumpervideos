What if you fold paper 42 times?
A sheet of paper is about a tenth of a millimeter thick.
Each fold doubles it. After ten folds, it is about ten centimeters thick.
After 23 folds, it would be as tall as the tallest building on Earth.
And after 42 folds, it would reach the Moon.
