Which bird flies the farthest?
The Arctic tern flies about 70,000 kilometers every year.
It travels from the Arctic to Antarctica, and back again.
Another bird, the bar-tailed godwit, flew 13,500 kilometers without landing once.
That flight took eleven days. No food, no rest.
