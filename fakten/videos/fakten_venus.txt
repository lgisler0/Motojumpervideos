Why is Venus so strange?
Venus is the hottest planet. About 465 degrees Celsius.
It spins so slowly that one turn takes 243 Earth days.
But a year takes only 225 days. So Venus needs longer to spin once than to circle the Sun.
And it spins backwards. There, the Sun rises in the west.
